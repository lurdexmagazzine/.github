# Voice & Copy Guide

## Tone

- editorial
- warm
- sensory
- confident
- refined
- never aggressive

## Language style

- short, composed sentences work best
- use words like: curadoria, ritual, elegante, premium, extraordinário, importado, casa
- prefer invitation over pressure
- keep the language human and natural

## Best practices

- describe the benefit in a way people can feel
- make the product sound chosen, not pushed
- let the copy breathe
- avoid over-explaining

## Avoid

- pushy discount copy
- slang that breaks the premium tone
- noisy punctuation
- excessive exclamation marks
- generic ecommerce phrases that could fit any store

## Sample direction

Good: `Uma escolha curada para deixar o cotidiano mais bonito.`

Better: `Curadoria premium para transformar o dia em ritual.`
