# Design System Notes

## Logo and typography

- Primary wordmark style: editorial serif
- Signature serif: **Cormorant Garamond**
- The MAGAZINNE companion should stay sans-serif with generous tracking
- Keep the mark elegant and spacious; never compress it into a generic retail logo

## Core palette

Use a restrained palette with a premium, editorial feel:

- **Aubergine Royal** `#2A1F38` — main ink / primary dark
- **Lilás Premium** `#B89AD6` — signature accent
- **Creme Magazinne** `#F5EFE6` — main background / breathing space
- **Lilás Imperial** `#8E6FB8` — deeper accent
- **Rosa antigo** `#E8B4C8` — soft beauty accent
- **Rosa profundo** `#C97A98` — alert / emphasis accent
- **Salva suave** `#A8C4A2` — home / care accent

## Layout rules

- Prefer editorial spacing over dense retail layouts
- Keep compositions to a small number of colors; do not use the entire palette in one piece
- Use thin lines and clean structure
- Product photography should stay rectangular or close to square; avoid rounded crops
- Keep the visual mood premium, calm, and buyable

## Copy rules

- Prefer **"Edição limitada"** instead of loud promo language
- Avoid all-caps shouting like **"OFERTA"** or **"PROMOÇÃO"**
- Avoid emoji-heavy copy inside brand assets
- Prefer concrete sensory benefits over generic hype

## Quick checklist

- Is the layout airy?
- Is the palette restrained?
- Does the copy sound editorial?
- Does it feel premium without looking cold?
